### Where High Fit % & Cost Savings Live

**High Fit % (percentage_high_fit):**
- Calculated in: `agents/agentic_fit/fitness_analysis.py::analyze_l3_fitness_with_llm()`
- LLM estimates percentage of L3 capability labor activities suitable for agentic automation
- DEFAULT_FITNESS_DISCOUNT = 25% applied (conservative estimate)
- Result stored in L3_capability_map field: `"High_Fit_%"` (alias), or `"High_Fit_percent"` (dict key)
- Injected into KPI generation as context

**Cost Savings:**
- Calculated in: `agents/agentic_fit/allocate_opex.py`
- Formula: `cost_savings_opportunity = labor_opex * (percentage_high_fit / 100) * (1 - discount_factor)`
- Where:
  - `labor_opex` = allocated OPEX × labor_percentage
  - `percentage_high_fit` = from agentic fitness analysis
  - `discount_factor` ≈ 0.5 (implementation/adoption risk)
- Result stored in: `capability_kpi["cost_saving_opportunity"]`
